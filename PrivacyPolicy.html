Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2025

HEXAGON DRAGON INTELLIGENT TECHNOLOGY PTE. LTD. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Smart Alarm mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

Important Notice: Smart Alarm is designed with privacy in mind. We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our alarm services and smart scheduling features. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Alarm Data: Alarm times, labels, repeat settings, snooze preferences, holiday preferences, and selected regions
  • App Preferences: Language preferences, notification settings, and other customization options

1.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (Device ID, identifierForVendor on iOS, Android ID on Android)
  • User Identifier: A unique user ID generated by the App to maintain your account across devices
  • Device Token: Push notification token for delivering silent push notifications to refresh smart alarm schedules
  • Timezone Information: Your device timezone and UTC offset to ensure accurate alarm scheduling
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the App, including features used and app performance data
  • Subscription Information: Subscription status, subscription tier (Free/Monthly/Yearly), purchase history, and renewal dates

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

  • Holiday Data: We retrieve holiday information from Google Calendar API to enable smart alarm scheduling that respects holidays in your region
  • Subscription Management: We use RevenueCat SDK to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect:
    • Device identifiers (Advertising ID, Vendor ID)
    • Purchase history and subscription status
    • App usage data related to subscriptions
    • Device and app information (OS version, app version, device model)
    For more information, please review RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Payment Processing: Actual payment transactions are processed through Apple App Store and Google Play Store, which have their own privacy policies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

2.1 Provide and Maintain Services

  • Schedule and trigger alarms according to your preferences
  • Implement smart alarm features, including holiday skipping and weekend adjustments
  • Send silent push notifications to refresh alarm schedules before holidays
  • Synchronize your alarm data across your devices
  • Maintain alarm functionality even when the app is closed (using AlarmKit on iOS 18+)

2.2 Manage Subscriptions

  • Verify your subscription status and tier using RevenueCat SDK
  • Enforce feature limitations based on subscription tier (Free/Monthly/Yearly)
  • Validate purchases and prevent subscription fraud
  • Process subscription upgrades, downgrades, and renewals
  • Synchronize subscription status across your devices
  • Provide customer support related to subscriptions

2.3 Improve Our Services

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve app performance and user experience
  • Identify and fix technical issues
  • Develop new features and enhancements

2.4 Communication

  • Send important service announcements and updates
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Send subscription-related notifications

3. Data Storage and Retention

3.1 Local Storage

Most of your alarm data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms:

  • SharedPreferences for app settings and preferences
  • AlarmKit for system-level alarms (iOS 18+)
  • Local notifications for alarm reminders

3.2 Remote Storage

We store the following information on our secure servers:

  • User account information (User ID, Device ID)
  • Smart alarm status (whether you have active smart alarms)
  • Subscription information (managed through RevenueCat)
  • Timezone and region preferences

RevenueCat Storage: RevenueCat independently stores subscription-related data on their servers to provide cross-platform subscription management. This includes purchase receipts, subscription status, and device identifiers. RevenueCat retains this data according to their own data retention policies.

3.3 Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active
  • After account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days
  • Aggregated, anonymized data may be retained for analytical purposes
  • Subscription records are retained for regulatory compliance (typically 7 years)

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols
  • Secure Storage: Data stored on our servers is encrypted and protected by firewalls and access controls
  • Access Control: Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular Security Audits: We regularly review and update our security practices
  • Device Security: Local data on your device is protected by your device's security features (passcode, biometric authentication)

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
